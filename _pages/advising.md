## Prospective PhD Students
____
A PhD requires significant effort: typically __5-6 years of focused study of a hard problem__.
Before applying, please make sure that you understand the potential opportunity cost of spending these years in a PhD program.
For example, [The Opportunity Cost of a PhD: Spending your Twenties](https://www.economics.utoronto.ca/public/workingPapers/tecipa-802.pdf) finds that PhD graduates earn significantly less than their peers early in their careers because they enter the labour market later,
and that the largest penalties fall on students who leave a PhD program late without completing it.

### Ideal Candidate
Our work spans embedded systems security, program analysis, binary analysis, and software supply chain security.
An ideal candidate has:
* __Strong systems skills__: comfortable programming in C/C++ and working with low-level software such as operating systems, firmware, and bootloaders.
* __Background in (or eagerness to learn) program analysis__: static analysis (e.g., LLVM/Clang, CodeQL), fuzzing, symbolic execution, and binary analysis.
* __Familiarity with agentic techniques__: using LLM-based agents to automate security tasks such as program analysis and vulnerability detection.
* __Ability to work independently__: you can take a loosely defined problem, break it down, make steady progress, and know when to ask for help.
* __Clarity in communication__: you can clearly explain your ideas and results, both orally (meetings, talks) and in writing (papers, reports).


## Current Purdue Students (MS or Undergrad)
____
We welcome current Purdue students who are interested in research. To work with us, you should have:
* __Strong systems background__: a grade of __A or higher__ in __ECE 469 (Operating Systems Engineering)__ and in __ECE 60852 (Holistic Software Security)__.
* __Time commitment__: willingness to spend __10-15 hours per week__ on research.

We may have funding for strong candidates.

For MS students, we prefer to work on __focused thesis problems__ rather than ad-hoc RA positions.
